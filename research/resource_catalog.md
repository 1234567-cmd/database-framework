## How to read this catalog

- **Authority** runs from 5 (official documentation, standards body, foundational peer-reviewed paper) to 1 (unverified).
- **Verified** means the researcher opened the page on 2026-11-07 or 2026-11-08. `no` means the URL came only from search results or could not be loaded.
- **Link check** is a separate scripted request on 2026-11-08. `bot_blocked_or_refused` and `not_checked_*` do not mean the page is gone; they mean the script could not confirm it.
- Page text was mostly read through a summarizing fetch tool, so treat wording attributed to web pages as paraphrase.
